## Communication Rules
- Primary communication channel: team group chat.
- Important decisions must be written in the group chat so all members can see them.
- Team members should respond to project-related messages within 24 hours whenever possible.
- A short progress check is held at least once per week and more frequently before major deadlines.

## Task and Deadline Rules
- Every task must have one clear owner and a target completion date.
- A member who expects a delay must inform the team before the deadline.
- Completed work must be shared with the team for review before it is considered final.
- Tasks that depend on earlier work cannot be finalized until the required predecessor task is complete.

## Quality Rules
- All diagrams, schedules, and descriptions must refer to the same project: E-Commerce Sales Analysis.
- Project documents must use consistent terminology, actors, objectives, and dates.
- Factual claims and dataset details must be verifiable and not invented.
- Final deliverables must be reviewed by at least one team member other than the original author.

## Missed Work / Conflict Resolution
1. The team first discusses the issue directly with the responsible member.
2. The task may be reassigned if the original owner cannot complete it on time.
3. Repeated failure to complete assigned work is documented in the team chat and reported to the team leader.
4. If the team cannot resolve a serious issue internally, the instructor may be asked for guidance.

## Agreement
By participating in the project, each team member agrees to complete assigned work responsibly, communicate delays early, review shared work, and support the team in meeting the final deadline.
